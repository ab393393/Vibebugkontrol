- Service summaries use icons and short text labels.

- The restaurant FSSAI explanation is expanded initially, with its qualifications retained.
- Home pest summaries keep descriptive content available on demand.
- AMC cards explain contract period, visit schedule and scope; they do not imply that any frequency or extra visit is automatically included.
