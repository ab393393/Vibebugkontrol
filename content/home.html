How much does pest control in Mumbai cost?

Pricing depends on the premises, pest concern, affected areas and agreed method. Ask for a quotation that identifies scope, visit frequency, follow-up and any exclusions. Confirm assessment charges and taxes before agreeing a service.

Is pest control safe for children and pets?

Precautions depend on the specific method and product. Tell the team about children, pets and sensitivities, and ask for preparation, ventilation, access and re-entry instructions. Do not assume every service has the same restrictions or waiting time.

How long does pest control treatment last?

There is no universal duration. The pest, starting conditions, method, preparation and continuing exposure matter. Ask what to monitor afterwards, what follow-up is included and how new activity will be assessed.

What is included in a restaurant or business AMC?

The quotation should state covered premises and pests, scheduled service frequency, preparation, records, exclusions and pricing. Emergency visits and add-ons need explicit terms; they are not automatically included in every annual maintenance contract.
